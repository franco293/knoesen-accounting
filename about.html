What SAIPA Registration Means

Why the letters after the name matter

"Accountant" is not a protected title in South Africa. Anyone may use it. Professional registration is what turns it into something you can actually rely on.

A qualification that was verified

SAIPA members hold an accredited accounting degree and complete a formal training contract and professional evaluation before admission. The designation is not self-awarded.

Ongoing professional development

Members are required to keep their technical knowledge current. In a country where tax legislation changes every Budget, that is the difference between advice and guesswork.

A code of conduct, and consequences

Members are bound by a professional code and are subject to a disciplinary process. There is somewhere to go if the work is not up to standard — which is not true of an unregistered bookkeeper.

Recognition where it counts

SAIPA is a recognised controlling body for SARS tax practitioner purposes, and a Professional Accountant (SA) is an accounting officer for close corporations and may compile annual financial statements.

You can verify any professional's membership directly with the South African Institute of Professional Accountants, and any tax practitioner's registration through SARS. We would encourage you to do that — with us and with anyone else you are considering.

How We Work

What working with a one-accountant practice is actually like

You get continuity

The person who set up your books is the person who prepares your annual financial statements and the person who answers when SARS queries something three years later. Nothing is lost in a handover, because there are no handovers.

You get honest capacity

A small practice cannot take on unlimited work without the quality dropping, and pretending otherwise would be the fastest way to fail clients. If we are not the right fit for what you need — a statutory audit, for instance, which requires a registered auditor — we will say so and point you in the right direction.

You get fees you can see coming

Scope is agreed up front so you know what is included before work starts. If something falls outside it — an unexpected SARS verification, a year of catch-up bookkeeping — you hear about the cost before it is incurred, not on the invoice.

You keep control of your own records

Your accounting data belongs to you. If you ever leave, you leave with complete records in a usable format. A practice that makes itself hard to leave is telling you something about how it retains clients.
