How We Work

What working with a one-accountant practice is actually like

You get continuity

You can discuss your bookkeeping, payroll, tax and year-end work directly with {{principal_first}}. Confirm the scope, who will handle each part of the engagement and how queries will be followed up before work begins.

You get honest capacity

A small practice cannot take on unlimited work without the quality dropping, and pretending otherwise would be the fastest way to fail clients. If we are not the right fit for what you need — a statutory audit, for instance, which requires a registered auditor — we will say so and point you in the right direction.

You get fees you can see coming

Each engagement is quoted individually. Agree what is included before work starts, and discuss how additional work — such as an unexpected SARS verification or catch-up bookkeeping — will be approved and charged.

You keep control of your own records

Agree how accounting records will be stored, accessed and handed over as part of the engagement. Available export formats can depend on the software used and the records supplied, so discuss these requirements before work begins or before changing accountants.
