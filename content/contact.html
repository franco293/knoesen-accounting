Before You Call

Common first questions

How much does an accountant cost?

It depends almost entirely on volume and complexity — how many transactions a month, whether you run a payroll, whether you are VAT-registered, and how far behind the records are. Rather than publish a headline number that would be wrong for most people, we scope the work first and quote against it, so the fee you are quoted is the fee for your business.

Can I switch accountants mid-year?

Yes, at any point. Your existing accountant is expected to hand over your records, and there is a standard professional courtesy letter between practices to make that orderly. Mid-year switches are common and rarely as disruptive as people fear.

Do I need to come into an office?

Not unless you want to. Documents can be sent electronically, SARS eFiling and CIPC are both online, and most clients handle everything by email and WhatsApp. In-person meetings are available across Nelson Mandela Bay by arrangement.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Client information is confidential as a matter of professional obligation and is processed in line with POPIA. Our Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is collected, why, who it is shared with and how long it is kept.
