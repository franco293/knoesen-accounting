2. How we try to keep it accurate

We take the accuracy of published figures seriously. Rates, thresholds and deadlines are taken from primary sources — principally National Treasury's annual Budget Tax Guide and the South African Revenue Service — rather than from secondary summaries, and each page carries the date on which it was last reviewed.

Despite that, we cannot warrant that every figure is complete, current or free from error, and tax legislation changes — sometimes mid-year and sometimes retrospectively. Where a page's review date is old relative to the current tax year, treat its figures as historical until they have been rechecked.

If you believe something on this site is wrong, please tell us. We would rather correct it than leave it.
