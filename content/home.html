What makes your service different from a larger firm?

You deal directly with {{principal}}, the principal accountant, rather than being passed between junior staff. That means one person who knows your business, answers your calls, and explains things without jargon.

I have not filed tax returns in years. Can you still help?

Yes. Bringing overdue returns up to date is common work for us. The sooner outstanding returns are addressed the better, because SARS administrative penalties recur every month a return stays outstanding and are charged per outstanding return, so several late years compound quickly.
