What this calculator does and doesn't cover

It covers the ordinary case well: salary or wages, the rebates for your age, the medical scheme fees tax credit, your own retirement fund contributions, and UIF. For most employees on a steady salary that is the whole calculation, and the answer should match your payslip closely.

It does not account for:

  • a travel allowance or a company car, both of which change the amount subject to PAYE;
  • the additional medical expenses tax credit for out-of-pocket costs, or for taxpayers over 65 or living with a disability;
  • commission, freelance or rental income — if you earn these you are probably a provisional taxpayer, and the calculation works differently;
  • retirement or severance lump sums, which have their own tables;
  • capital gains, foreign income, or a part-year of employment.

If any of those apply to you, the number above is a starting point rather than an answer. That is usually the moment worth a conversation — get in touch and we will work it out properly.
