The mistake almost everyone makes

To take VAT out of a VAT-inclusive price you multiply by 15/115 — not by 15%. Taking 15% off an inclusive amount gives the wrong answer every time, and always in the same direction: it understates the VAT.

On an invoice of R1 150 including VAT, the VAT is R150 and the value excluding VAT is R1 000. Take 15% off R1 150 instead and you get R172.50 — R22.50 too much, on a single invoice. Repeated across a VAT period, that is the sort of error that turns into an assessment.

When you have to register for VAT

Registration is compulsory once your taxable supplies exceed R2.3 million in any twelve consecutive months. Below that, you may register voluntarily once you are over R120 000. The compulsory threshold rose from R1 million in Budget 2026, and a great deal of South African tax content online still quotes the old figure.

Whether voluntary registration is a good idea depends on who your customers are — it helps if they are VAT vendors themselves, and usually hurts if they are the public. We go through both cases in the VAT registration guide.
