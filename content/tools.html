Why we built these

Most South African tax calculators want your email address before they will show you a number, and a fair few are still running last year's brackets. These do neither. Every figure is transcribed from the Budget 2026 Tax Guide, each tool shows the date it was last reviewed, and the arithmetic happens on your device — there is no server to send your salary to.

A calculator answers the ordinary case. It cannot tell you whether a travel allowance is structured sensibly, whether you should be registered for VAT, or why SARS has raised an assessment you disagree with. When you get to that point, talk to us.
