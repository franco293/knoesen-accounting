The guides on this site are general information, carefully sourced and dated — but general information is not the same thing as advice about your situation.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

1. General information, not advice

The guides, articles, rate tables and calendars on this website are published as general information about South African tax, payroll and statutory requirements. They are not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice, and they do not take account of your particular circumstances.

Reading a page on this site does not create a professional relationship between you and Knoesen Accounting, Tax & Payroll. A professional relationship begins only when we have agreed an engagement in writing.

2. How we try to keep it accurate

We take the accuracy of published figures seriously. Rates, thresholds and deadlines are taken from primary sources — principally National Treasury's annual Budget Tax Guide and the South African Revenue Service — rather than from secondary summaries, and each page carries the date on which it was last reviewed.

Despite that, we cannot warrant that every figure is complete, current or free from error, and tax legislation changes — sometimes mid-year and sometimes retrospectively. Where a page's review date is old relative to the current tax year, treat its figures as historical until they have been rechecked.

If you believe something on this site is wrong, please tell us. We would rather correct it than leave it.

3. Acting on what you read here

You should not act, or refrain from acting, on the basis of anything published on this site without obtaining advice about your own situation. Worked examples are illustrative and deliberately simplified; a real calculation would take into account other income, assessed losses, allowances, elections and timing that a worked example cannot.

Lourens Knoesen is a Professional Accountant (SA) and a registered member of SAIPA (South African Institute of Professional Accountants).

We do not perform statutory audits. An audit must be carried out by a registered auditor with the Independent Regulatory Board for Auditors (IRBA), which is a separate registration. We are also not financial services providers and are not licensed under the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act, so nothing on this site is a recommendation about any financial product or investment.
