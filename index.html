About the Practice

Accounting built around your business

Knoesen Accounting, Tax & Payroll was established by Lourens Knoesen in January 2021. Lourens is a registered member of SAIPA — the South African Institute of Professional Accountants — and brought more than ten years of accounting experience with him when he opened the practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions business owners ask before they hire an accountant

Good accounting should feel clear, calm and easy to understand from day one.

Do you work with small businesses and sole proprietors?

Yes. We support a wide range of SMEs, sole proprietors and growing businesses that need dependable financial administration and practical advice, from one-person operations to companies with staff on payroll.

Can you help with payroll and SARS submissions?

Yes. We manage monthly EMP201 declarations, bi-annual EMP501 reconciliations, UIF submissions to the Department of Employment and Labour, and SARS-related compliance work as part of our service.

Where are you based and which areas do you serve?

The practice is based in Walmer Heights, Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth). We serve clients across the Nelson Mandela Bay Metro — including Walmer, Newton Park, Summerstrand, Lorraine, Kariega and Despatch — as well as surrounding Eastern Cape towns. Much of the work is handled digitally, so distance is rarely an obstacle. See all areas →

What makes your service different from a larger firm?

You deal directly with Lourens Knoesen, the principal accountant, rather than being passed between junior staff. That means one person who knows your business, answers your calls, and explains things without jargon.

I have not filed tax returns in years. Can you still help?

Yes. Bringing overdue returns up to date is common work for us. The sooner outstanding returns are addressed the better, because SARS administrative penalties recur every month a return stays outstanding and are charged per outstanding return, so several late years compound quickly.
