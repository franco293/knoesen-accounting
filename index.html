About the Practice

Accounting built around your business, not the other way around

Knoesen Accounting, Tax & Payroll was established by Lourens Knoesen in January 2021.

Lourens is a registered member of SAIPA — the South African Institute of Professional Accountants — with more than ten years of experience in the accounting industry.

We specialise in comprehensive accounting, tax and payroll services for small and medium-sized entities (SMEs) across the Nelson Mandela Bay Metro and surrounding areas. We're based in Walmer Heights, Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), South Africa.

What We Do

Four ledgers, one accountant

From the first bookkeeping entry to the final statutory return, every service below is handled directly by Lourens — no call centre, no hand-offs.

The General Ledger

Accounting Services

Financial Statements

Compiling financial statements for companies, close corporations, individuals, partnerships, NGOs and other entities.

Bookkeeping & Record Keeping

Keeping your firm's monthly transactions recorded accurately and up to date.

Management Accounts

Preparing management accounts so you're kept informed of your firm's financial performance on an ongoing basis.

SARS & Revenue

Taxation Services

Income Tax

Income tax declarations for individuals, companies, close corporations, partnerships, sole proprietors and other entities.

Payroll & Payroll Taxes

Monthly and bi-annual payroll tax declarations to SARS, and declarations to the UIF.

Value-Added Tax

VAT declarations to SARS, prepared and submitted on time, every time.

Other Taxes

Declaration of other taxes as required by legislation, including Dividends Tax and Donations Tax.

The Registrar

Statutory Services

Annual Returns

Annual return submissions to the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).

Beneficial Ownership

Beneficial ownership declarations to the Registrar, as required under current legislation.

Registrations

Registering entities for Income Tax at SARS and for UIF at the Department of Labour.

Deregistrations

Deregistering companies and close corporations at the Registrar when it's time to close a chapter.

The Payroll Book

Payroll Services

Payslips

Accurate, on-time payslips for every pay period, for teams of any size.

Monthly Declarations

Monthly payroll tax declarations to SARS, filed correctly and on schedule.

Bi-Annual Declarations

Bi-annual employer reconciliations submitted to SARS.

UIF Declarations

Ongoing UIF declarations to the Department of Labour, kept fully up to date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions business owners ask before they hire an accountant

We believe good accounting should feel clear, calm and easy to understand from day one.

Do you work with small businesses and sole proprietors?

Yes. We support a wide range of SMEs, sole proprietors and growing businesses that need dependable financial administration and practical advice.

Can you help with payroll and SARS submissions?

Absolutely. We manage monthly and bi-annual payroll-related declarations, UIF submissions and SARS-related compliance work as part of our service.

What makes your service different?

You get direct access to the principal accountant, a personal relationship and a practical approach that focuses on clarity rather than jargon.
