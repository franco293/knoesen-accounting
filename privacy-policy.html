2. Our Information Officer

Under POPIA, every responsible party must have an Information Officer accountable for compliance. Unless another person is formally appointed, this is Lourens Knoesen as head of the practice. The Information Officer can be reached using the contact details above and handles all requests relating to this notice, including access, correction and objection requests.

Before this site goes live: the practice should confirm its Information Officer is registered with the Information Regulator (a short online process at inforegulator.org.za) and add the resulting registration reference here.

3. Information we collect

Through this website

We collect personal information only when you give it to us directly, by:

  • Calling, WhatsApping or emailing us using the details on this site;
  • Submitting the contact form, if enabled — typically your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message; and
  • Loading the embedded Google Map, which shares your IP address with Google (only happens if you click "Load Map" — see Sharing & third parties).

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not follow you to other websites — it records a page view, a referrer and a coarse country, and nothing that identifies you. We use no advertising trackers and no other analytics.

Please don't submit your ID number, tax reference number, banking details, or other sensitive information through the contact form — for that information, we'll set up a secure channel as part of onboarding you as a client.

Through a client engagement

If you engage us for accounting, tax, payroll or statutory services, we collect the personal information reasonably needed to provide that service — for example, identity and contact details, tax reference numbers, banking and financial records, and, for payroll clients, information about your employees necessary to process payroll (names, ID numbers, banking details, and remuneration information).

4. Why we process it, and on what basis

We process personal information for purposes that include:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing quotes;
  • Delivering accounting, taxation, statutory and payroll services under our engagement with you;
  • Meeting our own legal and regulatory obligations — for example, submissions to SARS, CIPC and the Department of Labour, and record-keeping required under the Tax Administration Act and the Companies Act; and
  • Maintaining our own accounting, engagement and compliance records.

Each of these relies on one or more of POPIA's lawful grounds for processing: your consent (for example, submitting the contact form), the processing being necessary to perform a contract with you (client engagements), compliance with a legal obligation we're subject to, or our legitimate interest in running the practice, balanced against your rights. Where an employer client asks us to process their employees' payroll information, we act as an operator on that client's behalf and process it only on their instruction and under confidentiality.

We do not collect special personal information (health, biometric, race, religious or similar data) through this website, and only process it in a client engagement where the engagement itself requires it and a lawful ground under section 27 of POPIA applies.

5. Children's information

This website is directed at businesses and adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. Where payroll or dependant information relating to a minor arises in the course of a client engagement, it is processed only as necessary for that engagement and with appropriate care.

6. Sharing & third parties

We don't sell personal information. We share it only where necessary, with:

  • Regulators and revenue authorities — SARS, CIPC and the Department of Labour, as required to deliver tax, statutory and payroll services;
  • Software used to deliver our services — accounting and payroll platforms used to prepare your records, statements and payslips;
  • Google Maps — only if you click "Load Map" in the Contact section, at which point Google receives your IP address and standard device/browser information to display the embed;
  • Web3Forms — if the contact form is active, your submission is relayed through Web3Forms' service to deliver it to our inbox. If you'd rather avoid this, use the phone, WhatsApp or email links instead; and
  • Cloudflare — Cloudflare serves this website and provides its analytics. Loading any page necessarily involves Cloudflare receiving your IP address, as it would with any hosting provider. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies and builds no profile of you.

Some of these providers may process information on servers outside South Africa. Where that happens, we rely on those providers' own safeguards and, where applicable, POPIA section 72's conditions for cross-border transfer (for example, the provider being subject to laws or agreements that give substantially similar protection to POPIA, or the transfer being necessary to perform our contract with you).

7. Cookies & this website

This website sets no cookies of its own — no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, and none needed to read it. That is also why you have not been shown a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. Fonts, styles and images are all served from this domain, so reading a page contacts nobody but us and Cloudflare, who serve it.

Two things reach further, and both are disclosed above: Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records a page view without cookies or any identifier for you, and Google Maps, which only loads if you click "Load Map" and may then set its own cookies — standard behaviour for their embed, and outside our direct control. You can block either using your browser's privacy settings without affecting your ability to read the site.

8. How we protect it

This website is served over HTTPS, and we apply security headers (including a Content-Security-Policy) intended to reduce the risk of the site being used to intercept or tamper with information in transit. For client engagement data, we apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards proportionate to a small practice — including restricted access, confidentiality in how we handle your records, and secure disposal when information is no longer needed — in line with POPIA's security safeguards condition (section 19).

9. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as it's needed for the purpose it was collected. Website enquiries that don't turn into a client relationship are kept only as long as needed to respond, then deleted. Client and engagement records are generally kept for the periods required by SARS and the Companies Act (commonly five years from the relevant tax year or transaction, sometimes longer for company records), after which they are securely destroyed or anonymised.

10. Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Be told what personal information we hold about you and why;
  • Request a copy of it (access);
  • Ask us to correct or update it if it's inaccurate, incomplete or outdated;
  • Ask us to delete or destroy it where we're no longer authorised to keep it;
  • Object to us processing your information on reasonable grounds, or to processing for direct marketing at any time;
  • Withdraw consent you've previously given, where processing relies on consent (this doesn't affect processing already carried out); and
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you believe we've handled your information unlawfully — see below.

Under the Information Regulator's current rules, you may raise an objection through any accessible channel, including email, phone, WhatsApp or post — free of charge. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details in section 1. We'll respond within a reasonable time and, where we can't act on a request, explain why.
