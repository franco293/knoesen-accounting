How these guides are put together

South African tax content online has a reliability problem. Figures get copied from year to year without being rechecked, and pages sit undated so there is no way to tell whether you are reading something current. As we write this, plenty of sites still state the VAT registration threshold as R1 million — a figure that changed on 1 April 2026.

So, three rules for everything published here:

  • Primary sources only. Rates and thresholds come from National Treasury's Budget 2026 Tax Guide and from sars.gov.za, not from other people's summaries.
  • Every page is dated. Each guide shows when it was last reviewed. If that date is old, treat the figures with suspicion — including ours.
  • Sources are linked. You should be able to check any figure yourself without taking our word for it.

These are guides, not advice about your situation — please read the disclaimer. If a figure here looks wrong to you, tell us and we will check it.
