Honest Scope

What we don't do

Knowing where a practice stops is as useful as knowing what it offers.

We do not perform statutory audits. An audit must be carried out by a registered auditor with the Independent Regulatory Board for Auditors (IRBA), which is a different registration from a Professional Accountant (SA). If your company's Public Interest Score or Memorandum of Incorporation requires an audit, we will tell you and help you find an auditor — and we can still prepare the underlying accounting records.

We are also not financial advisers. We will not recommend investment products, and we are not licensed under the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act to do so. Where a decision has both tax and investment consequences, we will handle the tax side and work alongside your adviser on the rest.
