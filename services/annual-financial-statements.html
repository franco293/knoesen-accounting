Annual Financial Statements for SMEs

Compiled financial statements from our Gqeberha practice, with remote service across South Africa and a quote tailored to your business.

Compiled financial statements for your SME

When you need annual financial statements, the starting point is your accounting records and the purpose of the statements. We compile financial statements for small and medium businesses, including sole traders and companies, with the scope agreed for your business.

Based in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), we can handle the engagement remotely for businesses anywhere in South Africa. You do not need to travel to Gqeberha to work with us.

Compilation only — no audit or independent review

A compilation prepares financial statements from the information supplied by the business. It does not provide audit or review assurance. We do not offer audits or independent reviews. If a bank, funder or other recipient requires either, a compilation will not replace that requirement. Tell us what the recipient has asked for before we agree the work.

The Scope

What we agree before work starts

The reporting period

Which business and financial year the statements cover, including whether earlier periods need attention.

The compilation

Preparation of the financial statements and related notes appropriate to the agreed reporting framework and engagement.

The state of the records

Whether the books are ready for compilation or need reconciliations, corrections or catch-up bookkeeping first.

Additional work

Tax returns, CIPC submissions and ongoing bookkeeping are separate services to discuss. Do not assume they are included in the compilation quote.

Records to have ready

The exact document list depends on your business. These are useful starting points; missing records can be discussed before you send anything.

  • Your financial year-end and previous financial statements, if available.
  • A trial balance, general ledger or the accounting records you currently maintain.
  • Bank reconciliations and statements for the relevant period.
  • Customer and supplier balances, loan records and an asset register where applicable.
  • Relevant stock, payroll and tax records, depending on the business.
  • Any specific reporting request from a bank, funder or other recipient.

For an initial enquiry, describe the records you have rather than attaching sensitive documents. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, ID numbers or banking details. We will agree how to provide the records needed for the engagement.

How remote preparation works

  1. Explain what you need. Tell us your entity type, reporting period, record-keeping system and any deadline.
  2. Agree the scope and quote. We establish whether compilation is suitable and what preparation or additional work is needed.
  3. Provide the agreed records. The engagement can be handled remotely, including follow-up questions about the information supplied.
  4. Resolve questions and complete the statements. Timing depends on the agreed work and when complete information is available.

What affects the fee and timing?

We quote individually. The fee depends on the business's complexity, the reporting period, the condition of the books and any additional preparation required. Several overdue years or unreconciled accounts involve different work from a current, reconciled set of records.

Tell us about a deadline at the start. We can discuss a realistic timeframe once the scope and records are understood; we do not promise a fixed turnaround or approval by a bank or funder.

Need the books brought up to date first? See our bookkeeping and accounting services. For a return alongside the statements, see tax services.

Your Questions

Before requesting financial statements

Can you prepare financial statements remotely?

Yes. We can handle the compilation engagement remotely for SMEs anywhere in South Africa, from our base in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth).

Do you offer audited financial statements?

No. We offer compiled financial statements only, not audits or independent reviews. Confirm the type of engagement you need before requesting a quote.

How much do compiled financial statements cost?

We quote individually after discussing your business, reporting period and the condition of your records. There is no published starting price.

Can I enquire if my books are behind?

Yes. Tell us which periods are outstanding and what records you have. We can discuss any catch-up bookkeeping separately from the compilation.
