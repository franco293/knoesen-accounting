The Payroll Book

Payroll Services

Staff paid correctly, PAYE and UIF submitted on time, and IRP5s that reconcile at year-end without drama.

Payroll is a deadline business

Payroll has the least forgiving calendar in South African compliance. Staff expect to be paid on the same day every month, the EMP201 is due within seven days after month end, and the EMP501 reconciliations happen twice a year whether or not anyone remembered them.

Miss a payslip and you have an unhappy employee. Miss an EMP201 and you have SARS penalties and interest. Get an EMP501 wrong and the error lands on your employees' IRP5 certificates, which means it lands on their personal tax returns — and comes back to you.

What we run

We process the payroll, produce compliant payslips, calculate PAYE, UIF and SDL, file the EMP201 monthly, submit UIF declarations to the Department of Employment and Labour, and handle both EMP501 reconciliations with the IRP5 and IT3(a) certificates they generate.

It works the same whether you have one employee or a hundred. The obligations start at the first hire — there is no small-employer exemption from PAYE or UIF.

The three payroll taxes, briefly

PAYE
Employees' tax withheld from remuneration and paid over to SARS monthly, calculated on the individual tax tables for the year.
UIF
Unemployment insurance contributions payable monthly — 1% contributed by the employer and 1% by the employee, based on remuneration below a prescribed ceiling.
SDL
Skills Development Levy at 1% of total remuneration paid to employees. Employers paying annual remuneration of less than R500 000 are exempt.

Rates per National Treasury's Budget 2026 Tax Guide, for 1 March 2026 – 28 February 2027.

Where employers most often go wrong

Treating an employee as a contractor. Whether someone is an independent contractor is determined by the substance of the relationship, not the label on the invoice. Get it wrong and SARS can assess the PAYE that should have been withheld — against the employer.

Forgetting fringe benefits. A company car, a cellphone, subsidised accommodation, a low-interest loan and employer medical scheme contributions are all taxable benefits with prescribed valuation rules. Leaving them off the payroll understates PAYE.

Letting the EMP501 drift out of sync. The reconciliation compares what you declared monthly, what you actually paid, and what appears on the IRP5 certificates. All three have to agree, and differences are far easier to fix in the month they arise than six months later.

Not registering at all. An employer must register for PAYE where employees earn above the tax threshold, and register with the UIF, within the prescribed periods after becoming an employer.

Questions

Payroll questions we get asked

When must PAYE, UIF and SDL be paid over to SARS?

The EMP201 declaration and payment are due within seven days after the end of the month the deductions relate to. Where the seventh falls on a weekend or public holiday, the deadline moves to the last business day before it.

Does my business have to pay the Skills Development Levy?

SDL is levied at 1% of total remuneration paid to employees. Employers whose total annual remuneration is less than R500 000 are exempt from paying SDL.

How much UIF must be contributed?

Unemployment insurance contributions are payable monthly, made up of 1% contributed by the employer and 1% by the employee, based on the employee's remuneration below a prescribed ceiling. Employers not registered for PAYE or SDL pay their contributions to the Unemployment Insurance Commissioner instead.

When are the EMP501 reconciliations due?

There are two each year: an interim reconciliation covering March to August, and an annual reconciliation covering the full tax year. These are what generate employees' IRP5 and IT3(a) certificates, so errors here surface directly on your staff's tax returns. See the deadline calendar for the dates.

Can I just pay someone as a contractor instead?

Only if they genuinely are one. The test looks at the substance of the relationship — control over how and when the work is done, whether the person is integrated into your operations, who carries the risk, and whether they work for others. If the substance says employee, SARS can recover the PAYE that should have been withheld from the employer, together with penalties and interest.
