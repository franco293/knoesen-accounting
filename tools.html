South African tax calculators

Free, accurate and private. Built on the 2026/27 figures, and the arithmetic runs on your device rather than our server.

Free calculators built on the 2026/27 figures from National Treasury's Budget 2026 Tax Guide. No sign-up, no email address, no adverts — and nothing you type is sent anywhere, because the sums run inside your browser.

Individuals

Income tax & PAYE calculator

Your monthly PAYE and take-home pay for 2026/27, with the age rebates, medical scheme fees tax credit and retirement contribution deduction applied — and a bracket-by-bracket breakdown of how the figure was reached.

Individuals

Bonus & 13th cheque tax calculator

What a bonus actually costs you in tax, and what reaches your account. Worked out the correct way — the tax on a year with the bonus less the tax on the same year without it — so a bonus that crosses a bracket is not overstated.

Net to gross salary calculator

Work backwards from the pay you want in your account to the gross salary it takes — and see what every extra rand of take-home costs in gross. For negotiating a package, or costing a hire.

Provisional tax (IRP6) calculator

Both IRP6 payments, with the basic amount and the 20% under-estimation penalty worked out — including the rule that quietly changes once your taxable income passes R1 million, which is where the basic amount stops protecting you.

Small business tax comparator

The same business taxed four ways — standard company tax, Small Business Corporation rates, turnover tax and sole proprietor. On one profit the difference can be threefold, and your margin decides it.

Companies & CCs

CIPC annual return fee calculator

What CIPC will charge, including every year you have missed — which is usually the number people actually need. Companies and close corporations are charged under different Acts, and lateness works differently in each.

Behind With SARS

SARS penalty & interest calculator

What outstanding returns are actually costing you. The administrative penalty is a fixed amount per return per month, and it runs whether or not you owe any tax — which is why it surprises people.

Open the calculator →
Employers

Employer cost-to-company calculator

What a hire actually costs, not what the contract says. UIF is capped, the Skills Development Levy has a cliff at R500 000 of payroll, and COIDA depends on your industry — so a flat "add 10%" gets it wrong at both ends.

VAT calculator

Add 15% VAT to a price, or pull the VAT back out of an inclusive amount using the 15/115 fraction — the step that most often goes wrong on a VAT return.

Why we built these

Most South African tax calculators want your email address before they will show you a number, and a fair few are still running last year's brackets. These do neither. Every figure is transcribed from the Budget 2026 Tax Guide, each tool shows the date it was last reviewed, and the arithmetic happens on your device — there is no server to send your salary to.

A calculator answers the ordinary case. It cannot tell you whether a travel allowance is structured sensibly, whether you should be registered for VAT, or why SARS has raised an assessment you disagree with. When you get to that point, talk to us.
